# Brand assets

The files in `custom_components/ford_connect/brand/` use unaltered Ford marks from the [Home Assistant Brands FordConnect Query asset set](https://github.com/home-assistant/brands/tree/master/custom_integrations/fordconnect_query). Home Assistant Brands distributes trademark imagery for product identification only and states that this use does not imply endorsement.

- `icon.png` and `dark_icon.png`: the square Ford wordmark asset.
- `logo.png` and `dark_logo.png`: the Ford oval logo asset.
- `icon@2x.png`, `dark_icon@2x.png`, `logo@2x.png`, and `dark_logo@2x.png`: the matching high-DPI assets.

The source asset set was selected because it preserves the Ford artwork without redrawing or AI generation. Ford trademark ownership and the integration's unofficial status are stated in the README.
